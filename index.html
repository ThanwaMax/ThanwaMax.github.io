Claude-Code-GWS

A personal, single-user Google Workspace integration.

Claude-Code-GWS is a private tool used by one person to access their own Google account from their own computer, using the open-source Google Workspace CLI (gws).

It lets the owner read and manage their own Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, and Tasks from the command line and with the help of an AI assistant.

This app is not offered to the public. It has no sign-up and no other users.
