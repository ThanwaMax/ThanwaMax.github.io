Privacy Policy

Claude-Code-GWS · Last updated: 11 September 2026

Who uses this app

Claude-Code-GWS is a personal tool with a single user: its owner. It is not offered to the public, and it only accesses the owner's own Google account.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app can access the owner's Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, and Google Tasks, along with basic profile information (name and email address).

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to carry out actions the owner requests, such as reading, searching, organizing, or sending their own email, files, and calendar events.
  • Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer.
  • When the owner uses an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) on their computer, the data involved in a request may be sent to that assistant's service to process the request.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with anyone else.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising.
  • It does not collect data from anyone other than the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
